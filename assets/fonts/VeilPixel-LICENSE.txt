VeilPixel.ttf - the pixel typeface of THE VEIL, now used for all text in Quarry Days.

Built by THE VEIL's tools/make_pixel_font.py: every glyph is rasterised, without anti-aliasing, from DejaVu Sans Bold at 10 px and
re-drawn as square-pixel contours (13 px em). It is therefore a derivative of DejaVu Sans Bold, whose licence (Bitstream Vera
Fonts Copyright + DejaVu changes, permissive, free to use / modify / embed / redistribute as part of software) requires keeping this notice:

The previous Quarry Days faces (Nunito Sans, Pixelify Sans, Silkscreen, VT323; SIL OFL 1.1) remain in this folder but are no longer used.
