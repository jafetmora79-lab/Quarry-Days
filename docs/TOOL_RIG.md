# First-person tool rig

* **FPHands** (`scripts/fp_hands.gd`): one shared pair of hands for every tool - forearm (two-bone arm to a shoulder below the screen edge, elbow bent out and down, wrist bend limited), wrist, palm, four fingers of three bones, a thumb of two. The fingers are wrapped analytically round the tool's shaft (each bone is a chord of the circle round it), so they touch the wood and never pass through it; palm on the arm's side, fingers round the rear, thumb across the front. Grip presets: `two_hand_shaft`, `striking`, `one_hand_hammer`, `bucket_handle`, `powered`, `precision`, `placement`.
* **FPTools** (`scripts/fp_tools.gd`): per tool, where each hand closes (point, axis, radius, preset, optional moving node such as the splitter lever). The tool decides where the hands go.
* **QuarryToolView** (`scripts/tool_view.gd`): swing styles - the shared strike (wind-up, strike with a short impact pause on heavy tools, recovery), `scoop` (the shovel: anticipation, insertion with resistance, lever, lift, carry, dump, recovery, impact at 34 %), `carry` (the bucket: weight sag, tip about the bail). Poses blend from wherever the last one left off (no snapping), with breathing, walking sway, recoil through the hands and a shudder after a blow.
* **ShovelPayload** (`scripts/shovel_payload.gd`): the heap on the blade, shaped and textured by what was dug (soil mound, gravel, rock chunks, ore). It appears only when a strike really collected units (`QuarryToolView.set_payload`, called from `main._player_yield`), stays through lift and carry and leaves in the dump phase; it never touches quantities.
* Evidence: `docs/previews/tools_sheet_1.png`, `tools_sheet_2.png` (idle / mid-use / recovery for every tool), `shovel_sequence.png`, `shovel_dig.gif`; regenerate with `tools/shot_tools.gd`, `tools/shot_shovel_gif.gd`, `tools/shot_grip.gd`.
* Tests: `tests/test_tool_rig.gd` (grips for every tool, fingers on the shaft, payload rules, bucket tip) plus the updated `test_tool_models`, `test_gear_models`, `test_animation`, `test_first_person`.

Known limits: the tools' Blender models are unchanged (the shovel blade was already concave with a socket); the hands are procedural low-poly, not a skinned mesh; the old Blender glove GLBs are no longer used in play; heavy strikes stretch the arms toward the target (the arm is drawn straight and slightly long).
