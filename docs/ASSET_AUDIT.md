## Repairs made at the source (Phase A)

- `MeshKit.repair()` (scripts/art/mesh_kit.gd): drops degenerate/duplicate faces and separates every pair of coplanar faces that belong to different materials by growing the smaller part 4 mm - a real thickness, not a depth bias. It runs wherever a generator turns soups into meshes (vehicles, rigs, tools, machinery, buildings), is deterministic, and settles chains over several passes. Cost: about +0.1 s at start-up.
- `MeshKit.tube_into()`: connected hoses/cables (no more rows of floating blocks).
- `tools/repair_glb.py`: Quarry Days copies of the Veil props in `assets/veil_qd/` (the pack itself is untouched): geometry baked into one mesh, duplicate faces dropped, flush faces separated, collision hulls kept, materials still mapped, brushed metal made matte. `MeshKit.prop()` loads the copy when it exists.
- `assets/materials/M_QD_MetalBrushed.tres`: the Veil brushed metal (metallic 0.75, no environment to reflect in the GL Compatibility renderer) as a matte version (0.2).
- Generator fixes: windows, signs, truck glass/mirrors/guard, excavator glass, jaw-crusher flywheel shaft, output-storage ladder, compressor handle and hose, pneumatic-chisel hose, hand-splitter bolt and lever, repair-part hose.
